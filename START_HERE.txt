VISIONPLAY - AI ROCK, PAPER, SCISSORS

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a writable folder.
2. Install 64-bit Python 3.11 if needed:
   https://www.python.org/downloads/release/python-3119/
   Choose Windows installer (64-bit), with the Python launcher enabled.
3. Double-click setup.bat. Internet is required for initial setup.
4. Double-click runme.bat. Click the camera window, then press SPACE.

Fist = ROCK    Open palm = PAPER    V sign = SCISSORS

Game works immediately with Google's pretrained neural model.
To demonstrate YOUR classical ML model: capture 1, 2, 3, then N;
repeat twice (three sessions total); press T to train, M to use SVM.
Press E for your real held-out test results. Q quits.

Read README.md for details and TEACHER_GUIDE.md before presenting.
These are plain text files; Notepad can open them.

No webcam photos are saved or uploaded. No administrator access needed.
Windows webcam/GUI must be tested on your laptop before class.
